# Credit Control and Differentiation Notes

## Business model

Spedite operates as a transport broker / 3PL: it receives bookings from companies,
pays vehicle owners or drivers quickly after delivery, and collects the freight amount
from the company later. The primary risk is financing a customer for 3–4 months without
security, which can turn payment delays or short payments into losses.

## Credit-control policy

- Approve a customer credit limit and payment term before accepting credit bookings.
- New or risky customers should pay in advance, provide a deposit, or pay before delivery.
- Give reliable customers short, defined credit terms; price longer credit at a higher rate.
- Stop further credit bookings when the customer crosses its credit limit or invoices are overdue.
- Invoice as soon as proof of delivery (POD) is complete, rather than waiting until month end.
- Reconcile deliveries, invoices, receipts, pending PODs, and overdue balances weekly for every customer.
- Maintain a dedicated recovery owner or team; do not leave recovery only to booking staff.

### Suggested ageing escalation

| Invoice age | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–30 days | Reminders and reconciliation |
| 31–45 days | Hold new credit bookings unless management approves |
| 46–60 days | Advance payment only; issue a formal recovery notice |
| Over 60 days | Stop service and send for recovery/legal review |

## Recovery documents

Keep a complete file for each trip: customer-approved rate, booking confirmation,
LR/bilty, e-way bill where applicable, invoice, signed or digital POD, delivery photo/GPS/time,
and relevant email or WhatsApp confirmations. Have a qualified Indian transport/GST adviser
review late-payment, dispute, suspension, and recovery clauses before using them in contracts.

## Differentiation

Sell reliability rather than only the lowest rate: verified truck placement, proactive tracking
and delay alerts, POD within hours, one accountable operations contact, transparent claims
handling, and quick reliable carrier payment. Specializing in a lane, cargo type, or industry
can improve margins more than competing as a generic broker.

Suggested promise: **Reliable capacity + instant POD + predictable billing.**

## Product capabilities to add later

Customer credit limit and exposure, invoice ageing, POD status, dispute status, carrier-payment
due dates, collections workflow, and per-trip profitability.
